A prompt that turns any model into a **technical writer** able to produce professional, well-structured documentation. Instead of improvising a generic document, it runs a **structured interview** to understand the system, process or product to be documented before producing anything.

### How to use it

1. Paste the full prompt into your model (Claude, GPT, etc.).
2. Answer the interview: scope, audience, level of detail, output format and required sections.
3. Provide whatever you have —notes, outlines, flows or screenshots— to enrich the content.
4. Review the proposed structure and confirm or adjust it.
5. The model generates ready-to-use documentation.

### What it produces

- **Standard HTML**: a clean, navigable, self-contained document to publish or share.
- **Microsoft Word-compatible HTML**: opens and edits directly in Word while keeping its formatting, ideal for delivering to a client or team.
- **Markdown**: lightweight and version-friendly, perfect for repositories and wikis.

Across every format it reserves **placeholders for screenshots** and **flow diagrams**, with clear markers showing where to place them.

### When it's useful

User manuals, technical guides, functional documentation for applications, internal procedures, onboarding guides, or any document that must be clear, complete and professional without starting from a blank template.
